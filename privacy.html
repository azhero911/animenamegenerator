Privacy & Data Standards

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to absolute transparency and zero personal data collection.

Commitment to User Privacy

Anime Name Vault is built as a lightweight, privacy-first static website. We firmly believe that searching for creative names should not require sharing personal information or being tracked across the web.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

When you visit and use Anime Name Vault:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • We do not track or log your clipboard copies.
  • We do not use advertising tracking pixels or fingerprinting scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties under any circumstances.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Anime Name Vault does not set tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. We do not require a cookie consent banner because no tracking technology is implemented.

3. Hosting and Server Infrastructure

Our website is statically hosted on modern edge infrastructure. Standard server logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) may be temporarily processed by the hosting provider for DDoS protection, network performance, and server security, in accordance with industry standards.

4. Contact

If you have questions or inquiries regarding our privacy policy or naming research, you may reach out through our official project channels.
