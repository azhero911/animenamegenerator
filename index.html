Frequently Asked Questions

How does this anime name generator select kanji for each name?

Every name in our database is paired with authentic Japanese kanji characters chosen for their historical validity and poetic meaning. We verify on'yomi and kun'yomi readings to ensure linguistic correctness.

Can I use these generated names for commercial manga, light novels, or games?

Yes. All names generated by this tool are free to use in your creative projects, including published light novels, webcomics, indie video games, and tabletop roleplaying campaigns.

What is the difference between romaji and kanji in the results?

Romaji represents the Japanese pronunciation spelled out using the Latin alphabet (such as Kazuki), while kanji represents the traditional logographic characters adopted from Chinese (such as 一輝), conveying symbolic meaning.

Does the generator include authentic Japanese surnames?

Yes. When using the default generator or the dedicated surname filter, names can include authentic clan surnames such as Takahashi, Fujimoto, and Minamoto.
