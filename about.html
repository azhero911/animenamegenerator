Our Mission & Standards

About Anime Name Vault

Dedicated to linguistic accuracy, Japanese cultural heritage, and clean ad-free tools.

Why We Built Anime Name Vault

Most online name generators produce random, disconnected syllables that make no sense to native Japanese speakers. Anime Name Vault was created to provide writers, novelists, manga creators, gamers, and roleplayers with an authentic onomastic resource.

Every name in our database is paired with valid Japanese kanji characters, verified on'yomi and kun'yomi readings, and poetic English translations. We believe that creative worldbuilding thrives when names carry genuine thematic and historical depth.

Our Core Principles

  • Linguistic Accuracy: We consult official Japanese joyo and jinmeiyo kanji dictionaries to ensure that every character combination follows real onomastic rules.
  • Zero Dependencies: Our site runs on pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. We load zero third-party tracking scripts, trackers, or external font stylesheets.
  • No Clutter: We do not overwhelm users with intrusive popup ads, cookie banners, or fake rating schemas.
  • Unrestricted Creative Use: All names generated on this site are 100% free for use in commercial light novels, video games, webtoons, and tabletop campaigns.
